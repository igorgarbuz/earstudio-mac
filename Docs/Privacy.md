# ES100 Control for iPhone - Privacy policy

ES100 Control does not collect data or send analytics to the developer.
It contains no advertising or tracking SDKs and requires no account.

Bluetooth is used to discover and control your EarStudio. Presets and preferences
remain on your iPhone. Device authorization is stored in the iOS Keychain.
Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Connection diagnostics are kept locally and leave the app only when you choose
**Share diagnostics**. Authorization keys are excluded. Review the text before
sharing. Files you export and external links you open are handled by the selected
app or service, under that service's privacy policy.

You can delete saved presets in the app and forget device authorization from
Diagnostics & about. Removing the app deletes its local app data; Keychain items
may persist, so use Forget device confirmation before removing the app.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the
[project's GitHub page](https://github.com/igorgarbuz/earstudio-mac).
Do not post private diagnostics or personal information in public issues.
