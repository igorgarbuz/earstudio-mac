# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Treat contributors, users, and the maintainer with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; explain concerns without personal attacks.
- Be patient with questions, differing experience, and volunteer response times.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's identifying information without permission.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and accept reasonable moderation decisions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For public conduct concerns, notify `@igorgarbuz` in the affected issue or pull request. If a report involves sensitive information, ask the maintainer for a private contact channel without posting those details publicly. GitHub's abuse reporting tools are also available.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock conversations, warn participants, or restrict participation according to the circumstances. Apply these expectations fairly and respect the privacy of people reporting concerns.
